Stelli

A night sky camera for Android. Stelli takes a series of long exposures on a tripod and stacks them on the phone into one photo of the stars, the Milky Way or star trails. It works without an internet connection, and your photos stay on your phone.

It needs a phone whose back camera gives apps RAW photos.
