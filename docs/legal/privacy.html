Stelli privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Stelli is a night sky camera app for Android, made by W&M Websolutions, a business registered in the Netherlands (KVK 98038672). This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: everything stays on your phone, and nothing leaves it unless you share it yourself.

Who we are

W&M Websolutions is responsible for Stelli (the controller, in the words of the GDPR). Questions about this policy or about your data: email info@siteaanpakkers.com.

No internet access

Stelli has no permission to use the internet. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party code that collects data. It cannot send anything to us, or to anyone else, by itself.

What the app uses, and why

  • Camera: to take the photos. The frames, the photos and the videos are saved on your phone.
  • Approximate location: only after you tap "Use my location" (in the planner, the sky calendar, the dark-sky map or the sky view) and allow it. It is used to work out the darkness, the Moon, the Milky Way, the night alerts and the dark-sky map for where you are. The app keeps the last place on your phone, so the planner opens without waiting for it. It is never written into your photos. You can type in a place instead and refuse the permission.
  • Motion sensors: to wait until the phone stands still before a shot, and to point the sky view.
  • Notifications: to show a session while it runs and, if you turn them on, the night alerts.

Your photos and sessions

Photos and videos you save go to your phone's gallery (Pictures/Stelli and Movies/Stelli). The frames of each session are kept in the app's own folder on your phone until you delete them. Uninstalling Stelli removes its own folder and settings, but not the photos and videos in your gallery.

If Android's backup is on, Android may include Stelli's settings, among them the last place used by the planner, in your own backup in your Google account. We cannot see that backup.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it. When you share a photo, a video or a debug report, you choose the app it goes to (mail, chat, a cloud drive), and what happens to it next is up to you and that app.

A debug report holds the app's logs, details of your recent sessions (settings, timings and file names, never the photos or frames), what your phone's camera can do, the phone's model and Android version, and its battery and temperature. It holds no location. If you send one to us, we use it only to find and fix the problem you report (GDPR art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). We delete it when the problem is solved, and at the latest 12 months after we received it.

Your rights

Because Stelli sends us nothing, we hold no data about you unless you email us or send us a debug report. For that data you have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability: email info@siteaanpakkers.com. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or to the authority where you live.

Children

Stelli is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page and in the app, with a new date.
